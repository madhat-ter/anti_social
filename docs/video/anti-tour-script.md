# anti — the whole tour (video script)

**This video tells** a curious stranger, or someone just handed an invite, **that anti is a
hundred-seat network built to give you people instead of a feed, and then send you outside.**

- **Length:** about 5 minutes (≈ 700 spoken words at 2.3 words a second, room to breathe).
- **Format:** 16:9 screen recording of the desktop site, with phone shots dropped in where the phone
  is the better view (tab bar, cassette, recorder). A 60-second cut is at the end.
- **Look:** the site's own: white paper, black ink, one red dot, System-6 windows, the pixel font
  for captions. No stock footage: every shot is a real screen.
- **Voice:** calm, a little dry, like a friend showing you round. Contractions. No hype words.
- **Capture:** the screenshot scripts already stage most scenes on a throwaway database
  (`scripts/screenshots-*.ts`). Record the same flows as video with real-looking members.

---

## 1 · Hook — 0:00–0:14

**On screen:** the landing page. The alert window zooms open (the System-6 "zoom rects"):
*"Are you sure you want to delete 'Social Life'?"* The cursor hovers over **Yes, I have an invite**.

**Voice:**
> Every social network wants more of your time.
> This one would rather you left.

*(beat of silence on the alert window)*

---

## 2 · The promise — 0:14–0:42

**On screen:** scroll down the landing. The hundred seats fill in, face by face, in their scattered
order. Then the three rules: **No DMs · No algorithm · Show up.**

**Voice:**
> This is anti. A small network with a hundred seats. Every face is a seat.
> You can't sign up. Someone inside has to invite you.
> There are three rules.
> No private messages: replies are public, and if you want to really talk, you meet.
> No algorithm: one feed, newest first, and it ends.
> And show up: if you disappear for a month, your seat goes to someone who will.

---

## 3 · Getting in — 0:42–1:12

**On screen:** the join page: invite code, a handle, a six-digit PIN. Then setup: the *About you*
box filling up, choosing a gender, a birthday. The doodle face draws itself from the handle.
Close-up on the face.

**Voice:**
> Getting in takes a minute. Your invite code, a name, and a six-digit PIN. That's your way in.
> Then you write a few lines about yourself: that becomes the top of your profile.
> And you get a face. Nobody uploads a photo here. Every face is drawn from your handle,
> so it's yours, and it's the same everywhere you show up.

---

## 4 · The feed — 1:12–2:00

**On screen:** the feed on desktop. Slow scroll. Call out, one at a time, with a red-dot marker:
- the line **"5 new since this past week"**;
- three cards that look different: a photo post, a short note set large, a long text that folds
  with *Read more*;
- a reply thread (smaller, on its own line); typing **@mi** and the member list opening;
- the **Like** button: on your own post a number, on anyone else's just a dot;
- a first post with **Say hello**;
- the end card: **"You're caught up. That's everything since Tuesday. Go outside."**

**Voice:**
> The feed is just everyone's posts, newest first. Nothing is ranked. Nothing is pushed up.
> A line shows where you left off, so you start with what's new.
> A photo looks like a photo, a quick thought looks like a quick thought, and a long one folds.
> Type an at sign and you can mention someone.
> Likes never move a post, and nobody sees your count but you.
> And then you reach the end. The feed actually tells you you're done.
> *(beat on the end card)* Go outside.

---

## 5 · Monologue — 2:00–2:48

**On screen:** the phone tab bar; tap **Monologue**. A cassette fills the screen: the kind in the
SIDE box, the title on the label. Tap it: the reels turn, the tape winds from left to right, the
time in the corner counts down, the waveform under the title fills in. Tap the **1×** sticker:
1.5×, 2×. Then **+ Record**: a title, *Thought*, the big Record button, the tape winding as the
person speaks, *Stop*, listen back, *Re-record*, then tick *This is mine to share* and post.
End on a profile's shelf of tape spines.

**Voice:**
> Some things are better said out loud. So anti has monologues.
> A song you wrote. A thought you've been carrying. Or just a yap about your Monday.
> Each one is a cassette. Tap it to play. The reels turn, the tape winds across, the time counts down.
> Five minutes at most. You give it a title, you listen back, and you can re-record as often as you like.
> Only your own words or your own songs. And once it's posted, it stays as you said it.
> Every member gets a shelf of their tapes on their profile.

---

## 6 · Collab — 2:48–3:32

**On screen:** the **Collab** tab. **+ Start** → three choices: *Event* (a picnic on Saturday),
*Activity* (a Sunday run club), *Project* (a zine made together). An event page: the ink ticket,
**I'm going**, a waitlist. An activity with *When we meet: Sundays 7am, by the lake*. A project with
roles to fill, **Ask to join**, and at the end *Finish* turning into portfolio drafts for the team.
Then the feed's end card again, now showing **Coming up: Darkroom evening**.

**Voice:**
> No private messages means the way to talk to people is to do something with them.
> That's Collab. One button, three kinds of thing.
> An event happens once: a time, a place, a list of who's going, and a waitlist when it's full.
> An activity keeps going, like a run club that meets every Sunday.
> A project has a team and a finish line, and when you finish, it lands on everyone's portfolio.
> Whatever's coming up shows at the end of the feed. Right where you're told to go outside.

---

## 7 · You, as a portfolio — 3:32–3:58

**On screen:** a member's profile: the portfolio window with the big name, the About, project pages
laid out like a portfolio, the shelf of tapes, the tabs **Posts · Collab · Picks**. The verified
badge and the admin tag next to names.

**Voice:**
> Your profile isn't a follower count. There are no follower counts.
> It's a portfolio: who you are, what you've made, the projects you finished with other people,
> and the tapes you've recorded.

---

## 8 · The small rooms — 3:58–4:28

**On screen:** quick cuts, about five seconds each:
- the **pick window** in the side rail: *Suggest a movie, a book, a place or something to eat*,
  then **Tried it** on someone's pick;
- **Pet Corner**: a doodle dog's page, a pet post with its *Advice* label;
- **Consultations**: a verified member's offer, **Book a consultation**;
- a **birthday** tag next to a name on the day.

**Voice:**
> And a few small rooms.
> Picks: a movie, a book, a place to eat, tried and vouched for by people you actually know.
> Pet Corner, because people's pets deserve their own page.
> Consultations: members with the verified badge, approved by an admin, can offer their time,
> and you can book it.
> And on your birthday, everyone sees it.

---

## 9 · Showing up — 4:28–4:44

**On screen:** the lurker banner with its countdown and progress (*2 of 3 done*), then the
waitlist screen: *You are number 4 in line.* The seat grid: one face fades, a new one fills in.

**Voice:**
> Seats are kept for people who are here.
> If you only watch, you're asked to join in. If you vanish, your seat goes to the next person waiting.
> Nobody owns a seat. You keep it by showing up.

---

## 10 · Looking after the room — 4:44–5:04

**On screen:** Admin: the reports queue (*hidden after five reports*), the verified switch, a
one-hour **visitor pass** being made, a visitor's struck-out buttons (*Visitors can only watch*).
Then **Settings → Dark**: the whole site flips to white ink on black. A Bangla post rendered in its
own typeface.

**Voice:**
> A few members look after the room. Five reports hide a post until one of them looks.
> They can hand a stranger a one-hour pass to look around: they can watch, and nothing else.
> Light by default. Dark if you choose it. And Bangla, in a typeface made for it.

---

## 11 · Close — 5:04–5:18

**On screen:** back to the landing alert window, *Are you sure you want to delete 'Social Life'?*
The cursor clicks **Yes, I have an invite**. Cut to the wordmark, **anti.**, and the red dot.

**Voice:**
> A hundred seats. One feed that ends. People you'll actually meet.
> Ask someone inside for an invite.
> *(beat)* Then go outside.

## Notes for whoever records it

- **Use real-looking members.** The test names (Mira Gurung, Ben Thapa, Ana, Sneho) and posts from
  `scripts/screenshots-monologues.ts` read naturally. Never record the live site's real members.
- **Show the phone where the phone is the point:** the tab bar, the cassette, the recorder.
- **Let the end card sit.** It's the whole idea; give it two full seconds of silence.
- **Things not to show:** a timer or screen-time lock (removed), header quotes (removed), anything
  that looks like a follower count or a ranking.
