# anti in one minute (video script)

**This video tells** someone who has never heard of anti **that it's a hundred-seat network that
gives you people instead of a feed, and then sends you outside.**

- **Length:** 60 seconds, about 115 spoken words (slow and calm; the pauses do the work).
- **Format:** 9:16 for phones (the cassette and the tab bar look best there); a 16:9 version can use
  the same cuts on the desktop site.
- **Look:** the site's own screens only: white paper, black ink, the red dot, pixel-font captions.
- **The full five-minute tour** is in `anti-tour-script.md`.

| # | Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00–0:07 | The landing page's alert window zooms open: *Are you sure you want to delete "Social Life"?* | Every social network wants more of your time. This one would rather you left. |
| 2 | 0:07–0:15 | The hundred seats fill in, face by face. | This is anti. A hundred seats. You only get in if someone inside invites you. |
| 3 | 0:15–0:27 | Scroll the feed: the "new since" line, a photo post, a short note. Land on the end card: *You're caught up. Go outside.* Hold it. | One feed. Newest first, nothing ranked, likes only you can see. And it ends. *(pause on the end card)* |
| 4 | 0:27–0:37 | A cassette. Tap it: the reels turn, the tape winds across, the time counts down. | Some things are better said out loud. A song, a thought, a yap. Five minutes, on tape. |
| 5 | 0:37–0:47 | Collab: **+ Start**, then an event's ticket and **I'm going**. | There are no private messages. Want to talk? Start something. A picnic, a run club, a zine. Then meet. |
| 6 | 0:47–0:54 | The seat grid: one face fades, a new one fills in. | Seats are for people who show up. Vanish for a month, and someone else gets yours. |
| 7 | 0:54–1:00 | The wordmark **anti.** and its red dot. | anti. Ask someone inside for an invite. *(beat)* Then go outside. |

## Notes for whoever records it

- Record on a throwaway database with made-up members (the screenshot scripts make them), never the
  live site's real members.
- Scene 3 is the idea of the whole site: let the end card sit for two silent seconds.
- Captions on screen, word for word, in the pixel font: most people watch on mute.
- Don't show anything that looks like a timer, a ranking or a follower count.
