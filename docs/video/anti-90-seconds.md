# anti in ninety seconds (video script)

**This video tells** someone who has never heard of anti **everything the site does, in ninety
seconds: a hundred invite-only seats, a feed that ends, tapes, Collab, portfolios, the small rooms,
and the people who look after it, and then sends you outside.**

- **Length:** 90 seconds. No voice (owner, 2026-11-11): the lines below are the on-screen titles.
- **Format:** 9:16 for phones, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.
- **Look:** the site's own screens only: white paper, black ink, the red dot, Archivo titles.
- **Replaces** the one-minute cut (`anti-1-minute.md`), which showed five of the features. This one
  shows every main feature in RFC §10; left out for time: notifications, search, the members list. The five-minute tour is `anti-tour-script.md`.

## Notes for whoever makes it

- Every screen is captured by `scripts/capture-video-90.ts` from a throwaway database with made-up
  members, never the live site's real members. The HyperFrames project that renders it lives outside
  the repo (`E:/work/anti-video-90`).
- Let the feed's end card sit; it is the idea of the whole site.
- Don't show anything that looks like a timer, a ranking or a follower count.
